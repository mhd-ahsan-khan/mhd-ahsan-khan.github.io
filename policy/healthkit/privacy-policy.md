# Privacy Policy for TodoSync

**Last Updated: August 30, 2026**

**TodoSync** ("we", "our", or "us"), developed by **Ahsan**, is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application and companion smartwatch app collect, use, and safeguard your information.

---

### 1. Information We Collect
To provide task management and wearable synchronization services, TodoSync may access the following data:

* **Task & To-Do Information:** Tasks, titles, completion statuses, and timestamps you create within the app.
* **Wearable Device Information:** Device name, connection status, and model of your paired Huawei smartwatch via Huawei Wear Engine SDK.
* **Fitness & Activity Data (Optional):** With your explicit consent, we may access your daily step count, calories burned, and activity records via Huawei Health Kit / Health Service to display fitness progress alongside your daily goals.

---

### 2. How We Use Your Information
We use the collected information solely for the following purposes:
* Synchronizing tasks between your Android smartphone and your paired smartwatch.
* Displaying your daily activity summaries and task progress on the in-app dashboard.
* Improving app functionality and connection stability.

We **do not** sell, rent, or trade your personal health or task data to third parties or advertising networks.

---

### 3. Data Storage and Security
* All task data and local sync queues are stored securely on your local device.
* Health and fitness data retrieved through Huawei Health Kit is processed locally and is not transmitted to external unauthorized servers.
* We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information against unauthorized access.

---

### 4. User Consent and Permissions
* **Wear Engine:** You can grant or revoke wearable device access at any time in **Huawei Health > Me > Settings > Device capabilities**.
* **Health Kit:** You can grant or revoke fitness data permissions at any time through your **HUAWEI ID Account Settings > Privacy Center**.

---

### 5. Children's Privacy
TodoSync does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.
