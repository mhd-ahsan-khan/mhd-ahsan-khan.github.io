Important Notice & Non-Medical Disclaimer

Wristlab Health is a personal health, wellness, and fitness tracking application. Wristlab Health is NOT a certified medical device and does not offer medical diagnoses, clinical treatments, or disease prevention services. All physiological metrics (including heart rate, SpO2, blood pressure, body temperature, and blood glucose) are provided solely for general fitness conditioning, athletic tracking, and wellness monitoring.

1. Introduction

Ahsan Ayub ("Developer", "we", "our", or "us"), an independent software developer, is dedicated to safeguarding your privacy and ensuring complete transparency regarding how data is collected, processed, and protected in the Wristlab Health mobile application (APK: com.wristlab.health).

By installing, connecting, or using Wristlab Health, you acknowledge that you have read and accepted the data processing practices outlined in this Privacy Policy.

2. Data We Collect Through Huawei Health Kit

With your explicit consent and authorization via the official Huawei Health Kit interface, our application requests access to specific fitness and health indicators recorded by your Huawei smart wearables (e.g., Huawei Watch GT series, Huawei Watch D series, smart bands). In compliance with the principle of data minimization, we access only the data necessary for the app’s features:

Data Category Specific Data Scopes Purpose of Use
Activity & Motion Step count, Cadence, Distance, Ascent & Altitude, Basic activity sessions To calculate daily activity milestones, visualize step goals, and generate workout logs in your personal fitness calendar.
Cardiovascular & Vitals Heart rate, Blood pressure, SpO2 (Blood oxygen), Body temperature, Heart health / Arrhythmia flags To monitor cardiovascular exertion during workouts, maintain a personal health recovery diary, and ensure training safety.
Metabolic & Lifestyle Blood glucose readings, Emotion / Mood indicators To assist users in observing how physical activity correlates with metabolic stability and emotional stress recovery.
Athletic Capacity Sport ability (VO2 max, running ability index, recovery score) To calculate baseline athletic fitness, personalize training intensity, and help prevent overtraining.
User Profile Age, Gender, Height, Body weight To compute individual Basal Metabolic Rates (BMR) and calibrate target heart rate training zones without redundant manual entry.

3. How We Use Your Data

We process the synchronized data exclusively for the following functional purposes:

  • Presenting your real-time and historical health trends on visual dashboards and charts.
  • Calculating customized fitness indicators, active calories burned, and training intensity recommendations.
  • Providing proactive workout safety warnings if your exercise heart rate exceeds pre-set thresholds.
  • Allowing you to maintain a unified workout diary across various sports (running, cycling, gym workouts).

We will NEVER sell, lease, or use your health or personal data for advertising, marketing profiling, or commercial resale.

4. Data Storage, Security, and Protection

We treat health and physiological information with the highest security standards:

  • Transmission Encryption: All data exchanged between your device, Huawei Health Kit APIs, and our backend is encrypted in transit using industry-standard TLS 1.3 / HTTPS with secure cipher suites.
  • Local Storage Encryption: On-device cached health records are stored in the application's private sandboxed database protected with AES-256 encryption.
  • Access Control: Access to systems is restricted strictly on a need-to-know basis and secured with multi-factor authentication.

5. Data Sharing and Third-Party Disclosure

The Developer does not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal health data with any third parties. We will only disclose your data under the following narrow circumstances:

  • With Your Explicit Instruction: When you voluntarily choose to export or share your fitness summaries with a designated coach or trainer.
  • Legal Requirements: If strictly required by law, regulation, subpoena, or enforceable governmental warrant.

6. User Rights and Data Control

You maintain full ownership and control over your personal data at all times:

  • Revoke Huawei Health Kit Authorization: You can disconnect and revoke Wristlab Health's access at any time through the app's Settings > Connected Devices > Huawei Health Kit > Disconnect, or directly in the Huawei Health app under Privacy Management > Third-Party Services.
  • Data Access and Export: You may request a copy of your stored records in a readable digital format.
  • Data Erasure (Account Deletion): You may request permanent deletion of your account and all associated health history via in-app settings or by emailing ahsanayub00@gmail.com. All records will be permanently purged within 30 days.

7. Protection of Children's Privacy

Wristlab Health is designed for individuals who are at least 18 years of age (or the age of legal majority in their jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal health data from minors.
