Data Subject Rights — FlipMatch

Last updated: August 12, 2026

FlipMatch does not collect or process any personal data on our servers. All game settings (difficulty, theme, sound, and haptics preferences) are stored locally on your own device and are never transmitted to us or any third party.

Your Rights

Because we do not collect, store, or process any personal data about you, there is no personal data held by us to access, correct, delete, or export. You retain full control over the data stored locally on your device:

  • Access / Export: All app data resides only on your device and is accessible only to you.
  • Deletion: You can delete all locally stored app data at any time by uninstalling the App, or by clearing the App's data through your device's system settings.
  • Correction: You can change any stored preference (difficulty, theme, sound, haptics) at any time directly within the App's Settings screen.
