Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Traffic Racer ("the App", "we", "us") handles information when you use our HarmonyOS wearable application. We built Traffic Racer to work entirely on your device, with no account creation and no data sent to external servers.

1. Information We Collect

Traffic Racer does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information. The App does not require an account, login, or internet connection to function.

The only data the App stores is kept locally on your watch, including:

  • Your selected vehicle (Car, Bike, or Truck)
  • Your game settings (starting speed, haptics on/off)
  • Your best score and most recent game result

This information never leaves your device and is not accessible to us, Huawei, or any third party.

2. Permissions Used

The App may request the following device capability:

  • Vibration/Haptics — used only to provide feedback when your vehicle collides with traffic. This can be turned off in Settings.

The App does not access your location, contacts, health data, microphone, camera, or any other sensitive device data.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share any data, because no personal or usage data is collected in the first place. There are no analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, or third-party trackers embedded in this App.

4. Children's Privacy

Traffic Racer does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13. Since the App stores data only locally and collects nothing personally identifiable, it is safe for all ages.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

All locally stored data (settings, best score, vehicle choice) can be cleared at any time by uninstalling the App from your watch, which permanently removes all associated local data.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.
