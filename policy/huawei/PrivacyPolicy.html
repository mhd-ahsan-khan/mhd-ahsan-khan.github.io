Privacy Policy for FlipMatch

Last updated: August 12, 2026

FlipMatch ("the App") is a card-matching memory game for HarmonyOS watches, developed by Ahsan Khan ("we", "us"). This Privacy Policy explains what data the App handles.

1. Data We Collect

FlipMatch does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. The App does not require an account, does not connect to the internet, and does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

2. Data Stored On Your Device

The App stores your game preferences locally on your device only, using the HarmonyOS Preferences storage system. This includes:

  • Selected difficulty level
  • Selected emoji theme
  • Sound on/off setting
  • Haptics on/off setting

None of this information leaves your device. We have no access to it, and it is never transmitted to us or any third party.

3. Permissions

The App requests the VIBRATE permission solely to provide haptic feedback when you match or mismatch cards during gameplay. This permission is not used to collect any data.

4. Children's Privacy

FlipMatch does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect personal information at all.

5. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes in the future, the updated version will be posted at this same URL with a revised "Last updated" date.
